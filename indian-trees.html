Iconic Native Trees of India

From the arid Thar Desert of Rajasthan to the humid Western Ghats, India's native trees have sustained rural communities, wildlife, and local ecosystems for centuries.

Desert Lifeline: The Khejri tree (Prosopis cineraria)—the State Tree of Rajasthan—survives extreme desert heat with taproots reaching 30 meters deep while enriching the surrounding soil with nitrogen.

Four Legendary Indian Species

  • Indian Banyan (Bargad): Famous for its aerial prop roots that grow downward into new trunks, allowing a single tree to cover several acres.
  • Sacred Fig (Peepal): A keystone species that provides year-round figs for hundreds of bird and bat species and thrives in urban environments.
  • Khejri (Shami): Revered in Rajasthan's Bishnoi conservation tradition for providing shade, fodder, and drought resilience in desert soils.
  • Indian Rosewood (Sheesham): A fast-growing deciduous timber tree prized for durable furniture and riverbank soil stabilization.
