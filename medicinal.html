Nature's Pharmacy: Healing Trees of Ayurveda

For thousands of years, traditional Indian medicine and modern pharmacology have studied tree leaves, bark, seeds, and resins for natural therapeutic compounds.

Eco-Friendly Crop Protection: Cold-pressed oil from Neem seeds contains azadirachtin, a natural biodegradable biopesticide that protects crops without harming soil microbes.

Notable Medicinal Tree Species

  • Neem (Azadirachta indica): Every part—from leaves to twigs and seed oil—possesses natural antibacterial and antifungal properties.
  • Indian Gooseberry (Amla): Produces nutrient-dense green fruits celebrated in Ayurveda for supporting immunity and digestion.
  • Arjuna (Terminalia arjuna): A tall river-bank tree whose pale bark has been studied for traditional heart-health formulations.
  • Moringa (Drumstick Tree): Fast-growing tree whose leaves are packed with iron, calcium, protein, and antioxidants.
