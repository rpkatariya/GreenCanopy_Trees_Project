Protecting & Restoring Our Green Cover

Deforestation, wildfires, and rapid urban expansion threaten global tree cover, making community-led conservation and smart urban forestry urgent priorities.

Historic Sacrifice: In 1730, Amrita Devi and 363 Bishnoi villagers in Khejarli, Rajasthan, gave their lives embracing Khejri trees to protect them from being felled—inspiring India's modern Chipko movement.

Effective Conservation Practices Today

  1. Miyawaki Urban Forests: Planting dense clusters of native saplings close together creates self-sustaining mini-forests in school campuses and cities within three years.
  2. Van Mahotsav Drives: Annual monsoon tree-planting festivals across India timed with July rains so saplings establish deep roots naturally.
  3. Paper & Timber Recycling: Using recycled notebooks and certified sustainable wood reduces commercial logging pressure on old-growth forests.
  4. Post-Plantation Care: Protecting young saplings with tree guards and watering them through their first two summers ensures a 90%+ survival rate.
