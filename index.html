Why Trees Are the Foundation of Life on Earth

Trees are the longest-living organisms on our planet. Beyond producing the oxygen we breathe, forests regulate local rainfall, cool urban heat islands, prevent soil erosion, and support millions of birds, insects, and mammals.

Did You Know? A single mature leafy tree produces enough oxygen in one season to support two human beings for an entire year.
